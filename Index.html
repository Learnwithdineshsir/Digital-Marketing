BLOCK 4 OF 5

Map your last online purchase

The old funnel says Attention, Interest, Desire, Action. Today's buyer does not move in a straight line. Three lenses help: the classic funnel, Kotler's 5As, and Google's Zero Moment of Truth.

Kotler, Kartajaya, Setiawan · Marketing 4.0

The 5As

Aware, Appeal, Ask, Act, Advocate. People are drawn in, curious, ask friends and the internet, buy, and then recommend or complain.

Google, 2011 · Jim Lecinski

ZMOT

The Zero Moment of Truth is the online research a buyer does after a trigger and before the purchase. Brands that are missing at that moment lose silently.

Classic funnel

AIDA

Attention, Interest, Desire, Action. Still a useful baseline. Compare it with your own map and notice what it leaves out.
